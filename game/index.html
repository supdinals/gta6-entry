PALMIRA BAY

You are Rio Vale, a bike courier with empty pockets in a sun-drenched port city. Smuggler-queen Mara Sol hires you for one small delivery. One job becomes five, the bay police take notice, and by sunset you will either own the tide — or be washed out by it.

How to play Neon Tide

WASD / Arrows — move or drive. E — steal / enter / exit a car. F or mouse click — punch, hold near a car to shoot while driving is automatic aim. Shift — sprint. Space — handbrake. H — horn. Esc / P — pause. Follow the gold marker on the world and the minimap. Crimes raise your wanted stars; hide away from police sight to cool down. Finish all 5 story missions to beat the story.
